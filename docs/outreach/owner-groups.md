# Regional owner groups — WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook

Tier 1, and the most valuable thing on the list: a group is a city or a region, which is
exactly the unit the app needs. Post in groups you are actually a member of. A stranger
arriving to drop a link is what group rules exist to stop.

Send the card or the car-screen photo **first**, then the message under it as its own
message. A picture with a wall of text attached is scrolled past.

## The message (English)

> I built something small for the drivers here. It is a live map of the other Teslas around
> you, running in the car browser: when one comes within 300 m, a button comes up and you can
> wave at them. Their screen says who waved and offers to wave back.
>
> Free, no account, nothing to install — it is one page. Set it up on your phone first at
> teslawave.app, then on the car screen tap "I already set this up on my phone" and type the
> six-character code your phone shows. That saves typing the address on the car.
>
> Your position is never written to a database — it lives in memory for a minute so the cars
> around you can be drawn, and it is gone. Your car is hidden after ten minutes parked, and
> there is an invisible mode that drops you off every screen in about two seconds.
>
> It only works if a few of us in the same region have it, which is why I am asking here
> rather than posting it everywhere. It is open source if anyone wants to look:
> github.com/Meg4mi/teslawave
>
> Independent project. Not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.
> TESLA is a trademark of Tesla, Inc.

## When somebody says they tried it and saw nobody

This is the moment the product is lost, and it will happen every time until a region has
density. Answer honestly rather than encouragingly.

> That is the honest state of it — there are not enough of us yet for a random commute to
> cross someone. The way it stops being empty is a few people in the same area keeping it
> open on the same roads, which is why a club drive is the real test. If you are up for it, I
> will say when the next one is.

Do not answer this with simulated cars, an inflated counter, or a promise about next month.

## Following up, once

One follow-up in a group, and only when there is something that is actually new: a drive
being arranged, the first crossing between two real drivers, a change somebody there asked
for. A second "just bumping this" is how the group stops reading anything you post.
